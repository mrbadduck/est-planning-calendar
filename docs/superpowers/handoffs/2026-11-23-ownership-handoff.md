The options were sketched in the closed PR #3: Identity Platform custom SMTP, or links generated by the Worker and sent through a provider. Either way, DNS changes are additive records on a sending subdomain at Hover (decision 0003).

## What helped, what bit

- **Local QA works against the real data.**
  1. Run `wrangler dev` in `proxy/`, with a copy of `proxy/.dev.vars` and `--var OVERLAY_MODE:live` (or off).
  2. Serve `web/` on :8080 and `gather/` on :8081 with live-server.
  3. Point each app at the local Worker with `localStorage.setItem('est-proxy-base','http://localhost:8787')`.

  Writes go to the real Coda, so they need Eric's OK, clearly named test rows, and cleanup afterwards. Eric signs in himself.
- **The app's Cancel/Delete/Unpublish confirm boxes are native `confirm()`,** which blocks browser automation. Stubbing `window.confirm` in the tab is the workaround.
- **Coda takes about 28 s to apply a write,** and everything here is designed around that (decision 0008). In the browser, background confirms took 30–50 s.
- **A local restart left the overlay's Durable Object alarm stuck in the past.** `record()` now re-sets an overdue alarm. It's worth a save-and-reload check on Cloudflare after `OVERLAY_MODE` goes live.
- **Stacked PRs:** merging bottom-up without retargeting each next PR to `main` (or deleting its base branch) lands nothing on `main`. Landing the head of the stack in one PR worked.
- **Checks worth keeping:** `scripts/check.sh`, the help guard, and per-task review followed by a whole-branch review. The final review of the overlay caught a real bug before release.
