## 2026-11-16
- **Events open as a page.** Clicking an event opens it as its own page instead of a pop-up. **← Calendar** or your browser's Back button takes you back to the calendar just as you left it. A copied link opens the event's page directly.
- **Status & next step.** On a computer, a panel beside every tab shows the five steps as a checklist, with who and when where the app knows; what's left before the next step, each linked to its field; the one next-step button and **⋮**; and the listing box with **Preview listing**. On a phone, it's a box at the top of the event, and the next-step button sits at the bottom of the screen.
- **Details is now Listing.** The first tab of an event is called **Listing**. A dot on it means something there is holding up the next step.
- **Needs approval.** In the List view, **Needs approval** shows only the events waiting for Tribal Council, with a count. Anyone can use it.
- **Preview any time.** **Preview listing** is now in the listing box, and it works at every stage. Before the event is on Eventbrite, it shows how the listing will look from your own fields.
- **Write the listing early.** Summary, Description, Free tickets and Address on listing are open from the draft on. You still need approval before **Add to Eventbrite**.
- **Capacity is now Free tickets.** It sits in a new **Ticketing** section: how many free General Admission tickets Eventbrite offers. Paid tickets and discount codes are coming later.
- **Every tab explains itself.** Under each tab's name, one line says what the tab is for.
- **Listing holds just the listing.** The **Listing** tab now holds just what goes on Eventbrite, in the order people see it: Title, Summary, Description, When, Where and Ticketing. **Public summary** and **Public description** are now **Summary** and **Description**.
- **New Planning tab.** **Planning Notes** is now **Planning**. It holds what only the planning team sees: Program(s), Leads, **Internal description** and the planning notes doc.
- **A clearer top of the event.** Next to the title is its program, in the program's color, and under it the first line of the internal description.
- **A more compact editor.** Smaller spacing, fields and buttons, so more of an event fits on screen. Phones keep big buttons.
- **Cleaner Listing tab.** The little key and the **Event** heading at the top of the form are gone. Point at the icon next to a field to see where it goes.
- **One icon per field.** Each field name now has a single icon: a crossed-out eye for private fields, a gray clock for fields that go on Eventbrite once the event is added, then a green check when it matches Eventbrite or amber arrows when your change isn't on Eventbrite yet.
- **Quieter saving.** The **Saved** label is gone. While something saves, the next-step button says **Saving…** (or **Still saving…** when it's slow); otherwise it's saved. Only a failed save shows a message. Proposing, approving, cancelling and deleting show in that button too.
- **Steps and label agree.** The label at the top of an event changes at the same moment as the steps, including **On Eventbrite**.
- **Fixes.** An event opened from a link before your sign-in finished no longer stays read-only. The listing preview keeps the summary and description as separate paragraphs. Venues added to Eventbrite from now on no longer repeat the city in their address.

## 2026-11-15
- **One next step at a time.** The bottom of an open event shows its five stages, from Draft to Published, with one button for what's next. Everything else is under **⋮**.
- **Leads take it to Eventbrite.** After approval, Program Leads can keep editing and put the event on Eventbrite themselves: **Add to Eventbrite** makes a private draft, **Preview listing** shows what Eventbrite has, and **Publish** opens registration. If anything is missing, the app says what.
- **Honest saving.** **Saved** now means the change has really landed. It can take up to half a minute, and the next-step button waits for it.
- **Field icons.** Each field shows whether it's private or public, matches Eventbrite, is needed for Eventbrite, or isn't on Eventbrite yet. Private homes start with the address for registrants only.
- **Delete before publishing, cancel after.** Leads can now delete an unpublished event (its Eventbrite draft goes too). After an update changes the date, time or venue, you're offered to email the registrants.
- **No lock on approved events.** Approved events no longer show 🔒 on the calendar: leads can keep editing them.

## 2026-10-01
- **Built for phones.** On a phone, the calendar is now a simple list of what's coming up, and the top of the page is a single line. Tap **☰** for your account, Help, Feedback / Ideas, the program year and which calendars to show, and the round **+** to add an event.
- **New: List view.** On a computer, **List** sits beside Overview and Calendar: the whole year as an agenda, day by day.
- **Your settings stick.** The calendars you show or hide, and on a computer the view you picked, are remembered on that device instead of resetting every visit.
- **Calendar settings.** Showing or hiding planning events and reference calendars moved into **⚙** at the top of the page, next to **↻** and **+ New event**.
- **Adding from the calendar.** Point at a day or lane and click its **+**. Clicking elsewhere no longer starts a new event.
- **Past events lock.** Once an approved event's date has passed, its details can't be changed and it can't be cancelled or deleted. Notes, sign-ups and Attendees still work.
- **Smaller fixes.** Venue type is a dropdown, the new-event form fits its content, icons name themselves when you point at them, and on wide screens Help sits beside the calendar instead of covering it.

## 2026-09-30
- **Safer saving.** A save now changes only what you changed. Before, editing an event right after opening the planner could clear its program, leads or venue, an edit could undo an approval made at the same moment, and closing an event mid-approval could stop saving until you reloaded.
- **Leaving mid-save asks first.** On a computer, reloading or closing the page while a change is still saving (or couldn't save) asks before you go.

## 2026-09-29
- **New: Help.** Click **?** at the top for step-by-step guides. Inside an event, **?** opens the guide for the tab you're on.
- **The i button is gone.** The color and status key it showed now lives in Help, under *Read the calendar*.

## 2026-09-19
- **Publishing moved into Details.** The Eventbrite listing (public summary, description, capacity) now sits at the bottom of an event's **Details** tab, under **Public listing**. The separate Publish tab is gone.
- **Better on phones.** Events open full-screen, and the Attendees list fits a small screen.

## 2026-09-16
- **New: Attendees tab.** See who registered and who signed up for a slot, filter the list, and email everyone from your own account.
